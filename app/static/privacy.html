Privacy Policy

Ravi's Accurate Tax Services — last updated September 2026

Ravi's Accurate Tax Services operates an automated assistant on WhatsApp that collects the information needed to prepare your tax filing. This policy explains what we collect, how it is stored, and how to reach us about it.

Information we collect

Depending on the service you select, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, Social Insurance Number, date of birth, residential address, marital and family details, residency and student status, income and deduction details, and any tax slips, receipts or supporting documents you upload.

How we use it

We use this information solely to prepare and file your tax return, to quote our fees, and to contact you about your file. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising or marketing purposes.

How it is stored

Your information is held in secured databases and document storage. Social Insurance Numbers are encrypted. Uploaded documents are retained so that we can prepare your return and support it afterwards, including in the event of a review or reassessment.

Sharing

Information is shared with the Canada Revenue Agency only as required to file your return, and only after you have authorised the filing. We use third-party service providers for messaging, hosting and document processing, who handle the data on our behalf and only for that purpose.

Your choices

You may request a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Some records must be retained to meet Canadian tax record-keeping obligations; where that applies, we will tell you.

Contact us

For any question about this policy, or to request access or deletion, email raviaccuratetax@gmail.com or call 647-300-1516.
