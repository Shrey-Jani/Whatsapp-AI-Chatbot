Frequently Asked Questions

What if I forget to attach a tax slip?

Don't worry — your tax profile is safe. The CRA automatically attaches missing slips on their end during processing.

How long does it take?

Most personal returns are reviewed and prepared within a few business days of receiving your documents.

Why is my refund different from my friend's?

Refunds depend on income, marital status, child benefits, carry-forward credits and provincial factors — every profile is unique, so direct comparisons aren't accurate.

How do I pay?

Payment is by Interac e-Transfer after you approve your estimate. All transactions are final — no refunds once processing has started.

Is my information secure?

Yes. Your details are handled confidentially and used only to prepare your filing.
